Boos n Ribs

Past Boos n Ribs Posters

Boos and Ribs is a free Halloween event and COUP’s first event of the year! It is held at Ida Noyes and includes – true to its name – booze, ribs, and a variety of activities. The Pub provides the booze, and COUP caters ribs and other food. Past Boos and Ribs activities have included live music from groups such as Dirt Red Brass Band as well as arts and crafts. Costumes are encouraged!
