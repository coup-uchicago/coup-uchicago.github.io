Past Snowball Posters

Snowball is UChicago’s annual winter formal! It takes place during Winter Quarter, traditionally at off-campus venues such as Rockwell on the River or the Chicago Cultural Center. Hors d’oeuvres and mocktails are served, and a dancefloor is provided! The dress code is formal, and tickets must be purchased in advance. Transportation to the venue is not provided.
